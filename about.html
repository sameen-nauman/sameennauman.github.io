04 Who I Am

About Me

Product Manager with a background in Economics and an obsession with understanding people.

I'm based between Lahore and Islamabad — a true nomad who's learned that the best thinking happens when you're not standing still. My economics background means I don't just see features; I see trade-offs, incentives, and second-order effects.

What drives me is building things from scratch. I love the 0-to-1 phase — the messy research, the scrappy prototypes, the first time a real user clicks something you built. I go deep on user personas not because it's in the playbook, but because I genuinely want to understand what makes people tick.

Inside a team, I care about creating the conditions for everyone to do their best work — clear priorities, no ambiguity, and space to move fast. Outside of work, I believe the best product people are the ones who never stop being curious about everything else.
