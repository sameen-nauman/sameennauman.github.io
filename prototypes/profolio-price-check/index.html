Rules implemented

  • Band needs Purpose, Location, Category (sub category) and Completion Status, plus a valid price.
  • BI is called when any of the four attributes change, never on price edits.
  • At or above the minimum: no warning.

  • No range, BI error or timeout: nothing shown, posting unaffected.
